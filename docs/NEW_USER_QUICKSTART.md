# Overview360 New User Quickstart

Overview360 is an audit readiness and evidence workspace. It helps your team organise requirements, people records, asset checks, private evidence, actions, reports, and internal audit packs.

Overview360 does not provide legal advice, safety advice, certification advice, regulator approval, or a guarantee that an audit will pass. Your organisation decides what requirements to track and what evidence is acceptable.

## First-Day Setup

1. Check your organisation workspace name and user role.
2. Open Requirements and create or import starter requirements.
3. Add owners, categories, risk levels, review frequencies, and due dates.
4. Add evidence criteria for important requirements.
5. Add people in Competencies.
6. Add competency types, then add or update person competency records.
7. Add assets in Asset Matrix.
8. Add asset check types and assign checks to assets.
9. Upload evidence in Evidence Vault.
10. Link each evidence record to the requirement, criterion, competency, action, or asset check it proves.
11. Review Dashboard gaps.
12. Create actions for missing, expired, overdue, or failed items.
13. Build an internal audit pack from selected requirements.

## Key Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Requirement | Something the organisation must satisfy, prove, review, or keep under control. |
| Subject | The person, asset, role, site, department, process, or organisation area the requirement applies to. |
| Control | The method used to satisfy the requirement, such as a competency, check, review, approval, or inspection. |
| Evidence | The private record or document proving the control. |
| Action | A task raised when something is missing, overdue, failed, expired, or needs follow-up. |
| Report | A view of readiness, gaps, evidence, actions, competencies, assets, or audit trail activity. |
| Audit Pack | An internal pack of selected requirements, linked evidence references, warnings, and open actions. |

## What Each Module Is For

### Dashboard

Use this as the daily overview. It shows readiness, attention items, open gaps, quick upload, and routes into the main modules. Dashboard customisation is currently local to your browser.

### Requirements

Use this to define what must be controlled. A requirement is not just a file. It should have an owner, category, risk, review frequency, due date, and evidence criteria where possible.

### Evidence Vault

Use this to upload, classify, link, archive, and open private evidence. Files are opened through temporary signed URLs in production. Evidence should not sit unlinked unless it is intentionally waiting for classification.

### Competencies

Use the People Matrix for person-by-competency status. Use the Competency Registry to manage the competency definitions. Link training or certificate evidence to the person competency record.

### Asset Matrix

Use this for vehicles, equipment, facilities, or other controlled assets. Add check types, assign checks to assets, complete check records, and link evidence.

### Actions

Use actions to fix gaps. Assign an owner and due date, add updates, attach evidence, then close with a completion note.

### Reports

Use reports to review requirements, evidence, competencies, assets, actions, audit packs, and audit trail activity. Reports are operational summaries, not compliance guarantees.

### Audit Packs

Use audit packs to select requirements, review linked documents and warnings, then export an internal CSV or print/save-as-PDF summary. Evidence files remain private.

### Audit Trail

Use Audit Trail to review important system changes and exports. It is available to suitable admin roles.

## Weekly Routine

1. Check Dashboard for red and amber items.
2. Review open actions.
3. Upload and link new evidence.
4. Check evidence expiring soon.
5. Review competency gaps.
6. Complete due asset checks.
7. Update requirement review dates where reviews are completed.

## Monthly Routine

1. Review requirement owners and categories.
2. Archive or deactivate requirements that should not affect active readiness.
3. Check unlinked evidence.
4. Review asset check history.
5. Review reports for missing dates, expired evidence, and open actions.
6. Build a small internal audit pack to test traceability.

## Common Mistakes

- Creating a requirement for each uploaded document.
- Uploading evidence but not linking it.
- Linking evidence only broadly instead of matching it to criteria.
- Letting people and assets exist without requirement context.
- Creating competency records without deciding who the competency applies to.
- Creating asset checks without a clear control reason.
- Treating Not Required as a way to hide unknown data.
- Importing records without stable employee, asset, or requirement IDs.
- Treating demo/local features as proof of production readiness.

## Simple Rule

Use this rule when deciding where data belongs:

Requirements define the obligation. People and assets are the subjects. Competencies, checks, reviews, and procedures are the controls. Evidence proves the controls. Actions fix gaps. Reports and audit packs show the result.
